Fleet Management — Class Diagram

The full object model for the Submarine Fleet Management System: one abstract base, two concrete submarine types, and the entities they own and reference.

inheritance (is-a) composition (owns) aggregation (holds) association (refers to)

owns vs. refers

CentralComputer and each Message in the inbox are owned (composition — destroyed with the submarine). partners, a message's sender, and the fleet's subs are references to fleet-owned objects — never delete through them.

the virtual pair

display() and updateMission() are pure virtual, so every menu action runs on a Submarine* and the right override fires. The base needs a virtual destructor for safe delete.

availability invariant

mission == nullptr is the single source of truth for "available". assignMission() and endMission() are the only paths that flip it, so the flag can't drift.

two open points

Confirm whether CentralComputer belongs to combat subs only (shown here) or all types; and whether "the missions its fleet manages" means a shared registry beyond the partners list. Both affect this model.
